Privacy Policy

Wortkarte for iPhone and Mac · Last updated 7 November 2026

Wortkarte is a vocabulary app for learning German. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer cannot see your words or anything else you keep in the app.

Your words and progress

Your words, folders, learning progress, streak, profile name and profile photo are stored on your device and in the private part of your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit), so they are available on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Only you can access them. If iCloud is turned off, they stay on the device.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You can use the app without signing in. If you sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and, if you allow it, your email address with the app once. They are kept on your device and in your iCloud to show your profile. They are not sent to the developer.

Looking up words

When you look up a word, that word (and nothing else about you) is sent to kaikki.org and de.wiktionary.org to fetch its forms and meanings. These services may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, under their own policies.

On the Mac you can choose Claude by Anthropic for lookups instead. This is off by default and needs your own API key. If you turn it on, the words you look up are sent to Anthropic under Anthropic's privacy policy.

Translations and stories on your device

Example sentences are translated with Apple's on-device translation. The Read game writes short stories with Apple Intelligence on your device. Neither sends your words to the developer.

Scanning text

When you scan a screenshot or photo for words, the text is recognised on your device. The image is not uploaded or kept.

Folder links

A folder stays private until you create a link for it. Anyone with that link can see the folder's name, its words, and your profile name and photo, and can add a copy of the words to their own app. Your learning progress is never shared. "Stop sharing" or deleting the folder turns the link off; people who already added the words keep their copies. Links are hosted by Apple's iCloud.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device. No notification service of the developer is involved.

Deleting your data

"Delete account and all words" in the app removes your words, folders, progress, photo and name from iCloud and from your devices. If you signed in with Apple, the app also asks Apple to end that sign-in; for this it sends a one-time code from Apple to a small server run by the developer on Cloudflare, which passes it to Apple and stores nothing. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple at any time in Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Children

Wortkarte does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
